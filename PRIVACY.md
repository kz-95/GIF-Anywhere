# GIF Anywhere Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

GIF Anywhere does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

## What the extension does

- It shows a GIF button next to the text box you are typing in. To do that, it checks which text box has
  focus and where it is on the screen. It does not read, store or send what you type.
- When you search for a GIF, your search words are sent to tenor.com to get results, and the GIF previews
  and files load from media.tenor.com. These requests are sent without your cookies. Tenor is a Google
  service; its own privacy policy covers those requests: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- When you pick a GIF, the extension puts it into the text box you chose. The site you are on then
  handles it like any image or link you paste yourself.

## What is stored

Only two settings, kept in your browser's extension storage and never sent anywhere:

- whether GIF Anywhere is on or off;
- for each site, whether you chose to insert GIFs as links.

Removing the extension deletes them.

## No tracking

No analytics, no ads, no account, no remote code. The extension talks to no server other than Tenor.
